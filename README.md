## About

**Silica** is a timeline-based application for managing tasks, events and schedules.

The main idea behind the project is a **scalable timeline** — a workspace that allows you to view and organize events across different periods of time without being limited to a traditional calendar layout.

Instead of forcing every task into a fixed daily or monthly view, Silica aims to provide a flexible timeline that can adapt to the amount and scale of your data.

Whether you are planning a few tasks for today or managing events across weeks, months or longer periods, the timeline should remain a clear and convenient way to understand what is happening and when.

## The idea of a scalable timeline

Traditional calendars usually separate information into fixed views: day, week or month.

Silica takes a different approach.

A **scalable timeline** is a continuous workspace where the visible time range can change depending on the user's needs. You can focus on a specific period for detailed planning or zoom out to see a broader picture of your schedule.

The goal is to make planning feel more natural:

- **Zoom in** to focus on individual tasks and events.
- **Zoom out** to see the bigger picture.
- Organize information across different time scales.
- Keep tasks and events in a single chronological workspace.

## MVP

The first version of Silica aims to provide the foundation for a simple and functional timeline application.

The MVP will focus on:

- Creating tasks and events.
- Displaying them on a timeline.
- Editing and removing existing entries.
- Navigating through different periods of time.
- Scaling the timeline to view schedules at different levels of detail.
- Providing a simple and clear interface for planning.

The primary goal of the MVP is to validate the core idea of the project: **can a scalable timeline become a convenient alternative to traditional calendar views?**
